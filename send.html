How to

  1. Press Choose the AetherSDR window and pick the AetherSDR window (the Window tab in the picker).
  2. On the Quest, open the waterfall page, press AE screen and type the code above, or open the link.
  3. Press Enter VR. The AetherSDR screen floats to your left. Point at it and hold grip to move it.
  4. Keep this tab open: closing it ends the stream. Video goes straight across your network; only the connection setup uses the public PeerJS server.
