Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a signal to tune it. Frequency runs left to right, newest at the front, history rolls away behind. Ridges are CW, the comb at 14.074 is FT8 on its 15 s cycle, the hills higher up are SSB. Simulated band: the real thing would take AetherSDR's TCI spectrum stream.
On a Quest: open this page in the Quest browser and press Enter VR. In VR the band sits on a table in front of you; walk round it. Point a controller at a signal and pull the trigger to tune. Thumbstick up/down = shrink or grow, left/right = turn.
AE screen: open send.html on the PC, share the AetherSDR window, then press AE screen here and enter its code. In VR, point at it and hold grip to move it.
